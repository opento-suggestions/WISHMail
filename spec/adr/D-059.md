## Context

A return receipt costs a scheduled transaction and a manifest submission, and D-29 had already fixed that the recipient pays nothing.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-59, verbatim:

> Return-receipt fee: one stamp added to postage; `hdr.rr` carries the request; the recipient pays nothing.

## Alternatives considered

- Charge the recipient — rejected: P-16.
- Fold the cost into every envelope — rejected: blind delivery is the base service (D-29), and an unrequested receipt would be paid for by everyone.

## Consequences

Postage is `weight + (rr ? 1 : 0)` (§7.5). `hdr.rr` carries the request; a settlement short of that is postage-due and the envelope is unstamped (§11.4). The recipient pays nothing at any step (§10.4, T-P16-2).
