## Context

v0.2 named a Sponsor who funded an agent's genesis, and the ratified actor set of D-25 did not include one.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-48, verbatim:

> No Sponsor actor; funding is a payment. Sponsored genesis survives as provisioning (§4.6). Closes v0.3 §19.11.

## Alternatives considered

- Keep the Sponsor as an actor — rejected: a stamp is a bearer unit, so whoever paid for it is not a party to anything the envelope records.

## Consequences

§3.9 names no Sponsor: funding is a payment, not a party. Sponsored genesis survives as provisioning (§4.6), whose payer may be anyone and whose product is coordinates, never secrets. Closes v0.3 §19 item 11.
