## Context

The Ontologic primitive and WISHMail needed one shared shape, or the correspondence between them would be decorative.

## Decision

Resolution, then postmark, then receipt: three proofs, each consuming the prior. That chain is the shared shape. RECORD, 2026-09-04.

## Alternatives considered

- Treat the proofs as independent records — rejected: forging or swapping a link would then break nothing after it.

## Consequences

Becomes §10.1, extended to four: the branch where no lane answers ends in an attempted-delivery slip (D-36). Consumption is by hash — a proof's inputs name the prior proof's hash — so the AAD is where the resolution proof enters the chain and the envelope identifier is what everything after names.
