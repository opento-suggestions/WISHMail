## Context

Two HCS standards were candidates: a schema registry (HCS-13) and a reputation-score standard (HCS-25).

## Decision

HCS-13 is agreed as the schema registry. HCS-25 is a candidate build. RECORD.

## Alternatives considered

- Ship schemas without a registry — rejected: a chunk has to name the schema version it validates against.

## Consequences

HCS-13 becomes §5.11 and `schemaRef` (D-90). HCS-25 did not survive: Q-13 is closed and §16.8 refuses a broker-computed trust score outright, because a score that cannot be re-derived can never touch a standing (P-12). §19.5 lists it as settled.
