## Context

§14.2 defines the `x402-usdc` leg and requires that the Postmaster name a facilitator that verifies and settles the USDC payment. Which facilitator was left to the deployment: §19.2 states that deployment pins are filled by the deployment and not by this document, L-11 records that a public facilitator serves `hedera:testnet` and none serves `hedera:mainnet`, and `STATUS.md` §4 carried the row as unfilled and §6 listed it as open.

FETCHED 2026-09-06 (`recon/hol-x402-recon-2026-09-06.md:573`, `recon/hol-x402.pins.draft.json` → `x402org-facilitator-supported`): `GET https://x402.org/facilitator/supported` returned 200 unauthenticated, offering among eleven kinds `{x402Version: 2, scheme: "exact", network: "hedera:testnet", extra: {feePayer: "0.0.9185802"}}`. `hedera:mainnet` was absent. The repository's own `docs/dev-tools/facilitators.md:317` marks x402.org "Testnet only", "Requirements: None".

FETCHED 2026-09-07 (`https://docs.x402.org/core-concepts/network-and-token-support#x402org-facilitator`, the page Sonic named): the x402.org facilitator's supported networks include `hedera:testnet` and no Hedera mainnet; the default Hedera testnet USDC is `0.0.429274`, 6 decimals. That page names no fee payer and no endpoint URL, describing x402.org only as "the default facilitator in the x402 packages" — the fee payer above stands on the 2026-09-06 live read of `/supported`, not on this page.

## Decision

The reference Postmaster's `x402-usdc` method names the **x402.org facilitator** — `https://x402.org/facilitator`, scheme `exact`, network `hedera:testnet`, asset USDC `0.0.429274` (6 decimals), facilitator fee payer `0.0.9185802`, no signup, key, or credit required. RECORD (Sonic, 2026-09-07, naming the source page).

The choice is a deployment fact. It is recorded in the Postmaster's price list as `methods[].facilitator` (§14.3), named in the conformance claim through the price topic, and stated in `LIMITATIONS.md` under L-11. It is not a pin of §1.6 and does not enter `spec/pins.json`, whose contents §18.4 fixes.

## Alternatives considered

- **Self-host a facilitator.** Rejected for this version: it is what a `hedera:mainnet` leg would require (L-11), and this version deploys on testnet only (§15.5). Nothing in the design changes if a later version self-hosts.
- **Offer only the `hbar` leg.** Rejected: §14.2 requires that at least one method the Postmaster offers need no pre-funded Hedera account (P-16, T-P16-1), and the `hbar` leg's buyer has an account already.
- **A third-party production facilitator.** Rejected on the record: zero mentions of Hedera in the x402 repository's own facilitator documentation, and the recon did not enumerate facilitators beyond it (recon O-10). Nothing rules one out later; this names the one that answered.

## Consequences

Closes the `STATUS.md` §6 open item and fills the `STATUS.md` §4 row. `LIMITATIONS.md` L-11 and its header table now name the facilitator instead of `[fill at deployment]`, and L-11's keyless-leg sentence (P-16) resolves to `hedera:testnet`.

Two limitations stand unchanged and are not softened by this decision. The upstream Hedera scheme states its replay rule as a SHOULD under `scheme_exact.md`'s MUST, so **the Postmaster's own durable record of settled payment references is what prevents a second purchase**, not the scheme (§14.2, T-P11-5, T-P11-6). And `hedera:mainnet` has no facilitator anywhere: a mainnet claim is not a claim against this version (§15.5).

Account creation for a keyless buyer remains the Postmaster's stamp transfer to a public-key alias (§4.6, T-P16-1), never the settlement — so a facilitator's refusal to create accounts at its own expense does not reach WISHMail (L-11).
