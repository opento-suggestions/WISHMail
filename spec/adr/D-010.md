## Context

A sender states what a resolution rests on; a Verifier finds out. Conflating the two lets an implementation quietly promote a claim into a fact.

## Decision

The distinction is written into the specification explicitly: the sender declares a profile, its trust class, and its endorsements; a Verifier appraises. Appraised never exceeds declared. RECORD.

## Alternatives considered

- Report one standing — rejected: it hides which party said what.
