## Context

A return receipt must be attributable to the recipient alone, and a lane's threshold key cannot deliver that.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-76, verbatim:

> ScheduleSign is the return-receipt mechanism for §10 (HCS-10 `transaction` op on the lane + the recipient's ScheduleSign; attribution by the signing account). Approved ahead of §10.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

The mechanism is a scheduled transaction the recipient signs (ScheduleSign): the HCS-10 `transaction` operation on the lane names the schedule, and attribution is by the signing account on the schedule's record. Approved ahead of §10 so that §10 could be written around it. Composed fully by D-78; the network facts that make it possible are D-77's.
