## Context

A DNS answer or an HTTPS document cannot be re-obtained as it stood, so a proof over one is not replayable from its locator alone.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-71, verbatim:

> Non-consensus profiles snapshot their inputs into the manifest (the RRset + RRSIGs; the `wishmail` field + document digest), so the proof hash recomputes from the manifest alone and what was read is witnessed; a Verifier re-obtaining the input now reports agreement or drift (F-5). The manifest is one HCS message on the sender's log; oversize snapshots degrade to digest + coordinates. Locators are structured.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

A non-consensus profile's manifest MUST carry a snapshot of the bytes the rule read, so the proof recomputes from the manifest alone and what was read is witnessed (§9.1, T-P6-2 — a manifest with no snapshot is rejected at `send`). A Verifier that re-obtains the input now reports agreement or drift as an observation, never a downgrade (§11.6, F-5, D-82).
