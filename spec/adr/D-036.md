## Context

First contact that goes unanswered leaves nothing behind unless something is built to record it, and silence then reads as refusal.

## Decision

F-6 carries the coprocessor's I-4 sentence forward: the attempted-delivery slip is a proof of absence. RECORD, 2026-09-04: "If it's the same sentence, we should bring it forward".

## Alternatives considered

- Record nothing on an unanswered first contact — rejected: expiry would be indistinguishable from refusal.

## Consequences

Becomes §5.9, §10.5 and F-6. D-80 fixed that the slip's manifest is published before the slip is returned and that a slip is never superseded. §11.4 applies the same rule to an unsigned receipt: `unclaimed`, and nothing more.
