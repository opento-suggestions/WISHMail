## Context

Three ledger tags were in play and one of them had no deployment, no token, and no plan.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-114, verbatim:

> **Two ledger tags: `hedera:testnet` (BUILD) and `hedera:mainnet` (defined, no deployment).** Sonic 09-06. `hedera:previewnet` dropped from §5.1 (`CHANGED`); §5.1, §15.5, §16.7 now name the same set. §15.5's cross-reference corrected from §5.2 to §5.1. Claude's previewnet-reset rationale was MINE and unverified; the ruling rests on Sonic's scope, not on it.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

Two tags: `hedera:testnet` (BUILD) and `hedera:mainnet` (defined, no deployment). `hedera:previewnet` dropped. §5.1, §15.5 and §16.7 now name the same set, and §16.7 says what an extension for a third would have to pin — its lanes, manifests, settlements and anchors, and that ledger's consensus as the witness of its postmarks.
