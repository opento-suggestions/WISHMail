## Context

Cross-ledger proof representation was an ambition from the start, and an ambition is not a deliverable.

## Decision

CLPR is named in Extensions and not built: ledger-tag proofs and postmarks, with no assumption that any two of them share a ledger. RECORD.

## Alternatives considered

- Build a cross-ledger proof format now — rejected: it opens only once a second ledger tag exists.

## Consequences

§16.7 is written so that nothing in §7, §8, §10, or §11 names Hedera except through the pinned standards and the tag. D-114 fixed the two tags; D-113 made an undefined tag a core refusal (T-P9-11). CLPR itself stays open in §19.3.
