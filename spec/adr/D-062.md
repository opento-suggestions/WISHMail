## Context

Chunk sizing needed numbers: how much rides in one HCS message, how much ciphertext is an ounce, and how heavy an envelope may get.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-62, verbatim:

> Constants: `CHUNK_WIRE_MAX` 1024 (one HCS message, no chunkInfo); `OUNCE_BYTES` 4096; `MAX_WEIGHT` 16. Measured wrapper: chunk 0 ≈ 260 B ciphertext, later ≈ 530 B (informative).

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

`OUNCE_BYTES` = 4096 and `MAX_WEIGHT` = 16 stand (§7.5). **`CHUNK_WIRE_MAX` does not**: this row sets 1024 and D-96 corrects it to 1000, because HCS-10's only size rule is 'exceeding 1KB → use HCS-1', stated nowhere in bytes and never in RFC 2119. The per-chunk ciphertext figures here were superseded by the recon's measurement of the real wrapper (ledger §H) and are informative in either case.
