## Context

Postage had to be priced by something, and every candidate but size would have made the price depend on who was writing to whom or on what was said.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-50, verbatim:

> Weight: `max(1, ceil(ciphertextBytes / OUNCE_BYTES))`; one price per ounce; postage-due is a recorded fact; service classes stay Q-11.

## Alternatives considered

- Price by content type, sender, recipient, or profile — each rejected: it is P-11 defeated, and the carrier does not read.

## Consequences

Weight is the only scale, and it is the post office's scale: the stamp knows how heavy a letter is and does not know what it says (§4.2). Postage-due is a recorded fact, not an error — the Postmaster does not carry it and a Verifier appraises it unstamped. Service classes stay Q-11 (§4.7, §19.3).
