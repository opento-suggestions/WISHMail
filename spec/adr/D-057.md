## Context

Provisioning costs the Postmaster real network fees, and charging for it risked making a paid service a precondition of using the protocol.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-57, verbatim:

> Postmaster-provisioning MAY be priced; published beside postage; never required.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

Provisioning MAY be priced, published beside postage on the price topic (§14.3), and is **never required** — the self-provisioned path of §4.6 exists precisely so that its price binds no one who brings their own keys. Whoever pays for provisioning pays for it (D-48); the product and the custody are the same either way.
