## Context

Determinism (P-3) requires that two Verifiers produce the same bytes, and a Verifier that reports drift, its own clock, or the mirror it read cannot.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-82, verbatim:

> The bundle splits into *evidence* (deterministic; digested; what T-P3-1 compares) and *observations* (drift, surface disagreement, later declarations, mirror integrity, appraisal time, mirror read; undigested; never bears on state or standing). Drift is a fact about the world at the Verifier's clock, and P-3 cannot digest a clock. §5.10 amended (`CHANGED`); `state`, per-proof standings, `offChain`, `requests`, `orphans` added to the bundle.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

The bundle splits. **Evidence** is deterministic and digested — what T-P3-1 compares. **Observations** — drift, disagreement between surfaces, later declarations, mirror integrity, appraisal time, the mirror read — are undigested and bear on no state or standing (§5.10, §11.6, T-P12-6). Drift is a fact about the world at the Verifier's clock, and P-3 cannot digest a clock.
