## Context

A receipt says the envelope opened, and no cryptography can establish that. Presenting the whole receipt as `math` would have been a category error.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-81, verbatim:

> Category honesty for the receipt: trust class `math` for what it proves (this key signed for this envelope after this postmark); `opened` is the signer's testimony, as on a return-receipt card. §10.6 tabulates proves/states for all four proofs.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

The receipt is split: **proves** (recomputable — this key signed for this envelope after this postmark) is `math`; **states** (`opened`) is the signer's testimony, as a signature on a return-receipt card is. §10.6 tabulates proves-against-states for all four proofs, and §11.8 keeps reconciliation reporting testimony as testimony. This is P-15 at the level of a single object.
