## Context

HCS-10 is a Draft standard. A build can conform to it, extend it, or quietly diverge from it while using its name.

## Decision

WISHMail conforms strictly to HCS-10 rather than extending it: envelopes ride inside HCS-10 `message` operations on HCS-10 topics, and WISHMail defines only what rides inside. RECORD, 2026-08-10.

## Alternatives considered

- Extend HCS-10 with a WISHMail operation — rejected: an extension is not the standard, and conformance to it is a claim only WISHMail could check.

## Consequences

Becomes P-9 and §1.2's "WISHMail defines no transport". Its consequences were worked out later: what "conform to HCS-10" means was fixed to a blob by D-92; the size line by D-96 (CHUNK_WIRE_MAX = 1000); the memo rule by D-94; the lane reading rule by D-95.
