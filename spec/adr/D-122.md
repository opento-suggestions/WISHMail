## Context

§3.5 promised that the Postmaster holds nothing it does not have to, and nothing in the document said what it does hold or for how long.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-122, verbatim:

> **§15.1 states what the Postmaster keeps and for how long** (`CHANGED`; Sonic 09-07: "edit section 15.1 to include the retention sentence"). The Postmaster paragraph now says: it keeps what it needs to finish an act it is midway through and nothing after — a settled payment reference until the payment can no longer land (§14.2); a purchase or settlement in hand until the transfer or chunks are on consensus or the request is refused; no envelope, chunk, address, or resolution proof once the carrying submission is witnessed; no record of who wrote to whom (F-1). Indicative, no MUST — retention is not observable from outside, so a MUST would have no test; §3.5's promise ("what it sees, what it keeps, and for how long are stated in §15") is now true. No L-15: §15.4's range and T-P15-2 untouched.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

§15.1 now states it: the Postmaster keeps what it needs to finish an act it is midway through and nothing after — a settled payment reference until the payment it names can no longer land, a purchase or settlement in hand until the transfer or chunks it names are witnessed or refused. It keeps no envelope, chunk, address, or resolution proof once the carrying submission is witnessed, and no record of who wrote to whom: that record is Consensus's, and anyone's (F-1). §3.5's promise is made true rather than asserted.
