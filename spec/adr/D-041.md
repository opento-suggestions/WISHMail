## Context

An earlier draft burned the stamp when it was affixed, which made an envelope that never reached the lane a loss.

## Decision

Payment settles at STAMPED — the affixing transfer. The stamp is consumed at SETTLED, when the envelope is delivered to the lane. Stamps may be bought with no immediate use, as at a post office. RECORD, 2026-09-04: "Exactly right."

## Alternatives considered

- Burn at STAMPED — rejected: it makes an orphan a loss rather than a letter still on the counter.

## Consequences

Becomes §8.3's machine and §4.3's "affixed when the transfer executes and consumed when the envelope it settles reaches SETTLED". D-52 followed from it: an orphan needs no refund and does not expire. D-130 corrected L-13, which had said "consumed when affixed" against five sections that place consumption at SETTLED.
