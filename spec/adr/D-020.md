## Context

Whether the stamp should be a fungible token or a non-fungible one was being argued on aesthetics.

## Decision

The question is settled by measurement — cost, and what the fee mechanism actually admits — not by argument. RECORD.

## Alternatives considered

- Decide on the collectible framing — rejected as an argument, which is the point of this decision.

## Consequences

The measurement arrived with HIP-991: a custom fee is payable in HBAR or a fungible HTS token only. D-49 closed Q-12 as fungible on that ground, and D-20's method is what made D-49 short. §14.4 and L-12 state the consequence: a spent unit has no history to resell.
