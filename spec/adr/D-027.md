## Context

The surface had five verbs and no way for a recipient to sign for anything, and it was unclear who produced a postmark.

## Decision

`ack` becomes the sixth tool. The postmark is reclassified as an output of Consensus that `send` relays; it is not something a tool produces. RECORD, 2026-09-04.

## Alternatives considered

- Fold acknowledgment into `inbox` — rejected: opening and signing for are different acts by different rules.

## Consequences

Becomes §6.6 and the second half of P-2 (nothing the Postmaster says is a postmark). D-58 froze the six names; D-79 made chunk 0 the proof of posting whose witness the postmark is.
