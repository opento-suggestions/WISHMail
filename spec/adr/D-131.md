## Decision

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

The markers are stripped and the convention begins at this commit: 0.5.0 is the initial committed text, and from here a marker means a change against something a reader can actually check out. Claude's call, reversible, and the reason `CHANGED` markers appear nowhere in the frozen document (§1).
