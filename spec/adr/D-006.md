## Context

An agent messaging layer can be a mailbox or a megaphone. The two have different failure modes and different law behind them.

## Decision

A stamp buys one envelope to one witnessed-resolved address. There is no unaddressed mail and no broadcast. RECORD; grounded in 39 CFR §310.1.

## Alternatives considered

- A broadcast tier — rejected: an unaddressed envelope has no resolution to bind, so P-1 has nothing to weld to.

## Consequences

Becomes P-10 and §1.2. Grounded informatively in §17.2. Listed in §19.5 as not open.
