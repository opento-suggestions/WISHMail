## Context

'The lane between two agents' is ambiguous whenever more than one connection topic exists between them, and reconciliation cannot be deterministic while it is.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-60, verbatim:

> The lane, deterministically (§7.1): HCS-10 connection topic between two agents; threshold of their two keys only; no custom fee; earliest-created open lane is *the* lane; must be born from the doorbell the resolution proof yielded; for `send`, only a lane the sender's current key can sign.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

§7.1 fixes it: an HCS-10 connection topic, threshold of the two agents' keys and no other, no custom fee, born from the doorbell the resolution yielded, and *the* lane is the earliest-created open one. A Verifier discovers lanes by the same rule from the same doorbell, which is what makes P-3 hold. For `send`, a lane keyed to a key the sender no longer holds is no lane (§7.6).
