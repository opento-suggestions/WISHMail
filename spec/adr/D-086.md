## Context

Standing had been described with more words than it had values, and each extra word was a condition a Verifier would have to decide rather than compute.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-86, verbatim:

> Standings are exactly four — verified, unverified, unstamped, unbound. Postage-due is a reason under unstamped; timed-out is the slip's endorsement; unclaimed is a receipt status. §6.7's sentence tightened (`CHANGED`).

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

Exactly four: `verified`, `unverified`, `unstamped`, `unbound` (§6.7, §11.5). Everything else is a reason or a status beside them — postage-due is a reason under unstamped, `timed-out` is the slip's endorsement, `unclaimed` is a receipt status. Four values, an order over them, and reasons that name tests.
