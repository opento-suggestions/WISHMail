## Context

A settlement can be affixed to an envelope that never reaches the lane. Whether that is a loss, and whether it expires, had to be settled before F-3 could be written.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-52, verbatim:

> F-3 orphans need no refund: an orphaned settlement stays affixed to its envelope; the sender may submit it later.

## Alternatives considered

- Refund an orphaned settlement — rejected: it would require the Postmaster to judge that an envelope will never be sent, which nothing on consensus can establish.

## Consequences

An orphan is STAMPED and does not expire: the stamps stay affixed to that envelope and to no other, and the sender may submit it at any later time (§8.6). Postage is spent, not lost — the letter is stamped and still on the counter. A Verifier reports it under `orphans` (§5.10, §11.4).
