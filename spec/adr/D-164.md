# D-164 — The `register` operation carries the pinned fields and the deployed ones

Status:      accepted
Date:        2026-09-09
Decided by:  Sonic
Drafted by:  Claude Code
Sections:    none — no specification sentence changes
Tests:       none added, moved or amended. T-P13-4 is the court.
Supersedes:  none
Spec marker: none (a reference-implementation decision; ledger §B and §H carry it)

## Context

The 2026-09-09 anchor check (ledger §H, and the 09-08 census before it) found the
HOL testnet anchor `0.0.6913983` and the pinned HCS-10 text disagreeing about what
a `register` operation looks like:

- **The pin** (blob `0cb5d2eb6b98e12e4b44fa8c4fea6e10937b615a` at commit
  `7046156c`, `index.md:404-421`) gives `{p, op, account_id, m?}` with `p`, `op`
  and **`account_id` all Required**, and a field table naming no `uaid` and no
  `t_id`.
- **The deployment** carries `{p, op, uaid, t_id}` and **no `account_id`** in 350
  of the anchor's 380 messages, including its three newest.

WISHMail emits the pinned shape, because T-P13-4 reads "a `register` operation on
the anchor whose payer and `account_id` are both the agent's". The open question
was whether it may emit anything *beside* the pinned fields, or whether strictness
to the pin means the four fields and no more.

**FETCHED 2026-09-09** (Claude Code), the pinned blob fetched at the pinned commit
and verified two ways: `git hash-object` reproduces `0cb5d2eb…` and `sha256sum`
reproduces `688372d3…`, both exactly as `spec/pins.json` records them. Searched in
full for a prohibition:

- No validation section, anywhere in the document.
- No "additional properties" sentence, no "MUST NOT include", no statement that an
  operation carries only the fields its table lists.
- The only occurrences of "additional" are about topic-memo parameters
  (`index.md:150, 159`) and a registry's optional `metadataTopicId` (`:190`).
- **`uaid` appears nowhere in the document at all.**
- **`t_id` appears only in the `migrate` operation** (`:453, :464`), where it is
  "the new Topic ID to migrate messages to" and required there. It is not a
  `register` field in the pinned text.

So the deployed anchor has borrowed `t_id` into `register` with a different
meaning and invented `uaid` outright, and the pin permits neither and forbids
nothing.

## Decision

`register_agent` emits:

```json
{ "p": "hcs-10", "op": "register", "account_id": "0.0.N",
  "uaid": "uaid:aid:…", "t_id": "0.0.M", "m": "…" }
```

The pinned fields, because T-P13-4 reads `account_id` and §9.5 locates a
registration by it; plus the two fields the deployed readers parse, so that the
incumbents' tooling sees us in the shape it already handles. Strict to the pin,
legible to the deployed. Additive interoperability, and not a divergence: we emit
a superset of the pinned shape and never a substitute for it.

Had the pin forbidden additional fields, `register_agent` would emit the pinned
four and the finding would stand in §H as a divergence we accept. It does not
forbid them, so there is nothing to accept.

## Alternatives considered

- **Emit the pinned four only.** MINE, and it was the 0.5.5 position. Loses
  nothing in conformance and loses the incumbents' readers, which parse `uaid`
  and `t_id` and would show a WISHMail agent as an entry with no identity.
- **Emit the deployed shape only** — `{p, op, uaid, t_id}`, no `account_id`.
  Rejected outright: it fails T-P13-4, and it is the shape that makes every
  agent currently on that anchor resolve `blurred` under §9.5, since a reader has
  no account to check the payer against.
- **Emit both and let a flag choose.** Rejected (MINE): a configuration switch
  over what a registration *is* would put two different agents on one anchor under
  one release, and §9.5's two routes already locate either.

## Consequences

- `register_agent` — the §4.6 affordance, not a §6.1 verb — emits six fields.
- No specification sentence changes. §9.5 already locates a registration "by a
  `uaid` whose identifier and `nativeId` are the address's, **or** by an
  `account_id` equal to the address's `nativeId` account", so both routes find us
  and neither had to be widened.
- Ledger §H gains the fetch, dated, with the line numbers for the absence as well
  as the presence — an absence is a fact about a pinned document and is worth
  recording with the same discipline as a quotation.
- `t_id` in our `register` carries the agent's HCS-2 declaration registry, which
  is what the deployed readers expect there and what §9.5's route (a) follows. The
  pin's own `migrate` meaning is not ours and does not apply on a `register`.
