## Context

The first design question WISHMail had to answer: where a resolution proof lives relative to the envelope it addresses. Carried alongside it, a proof can be stripped, swapped, or forged without the envelope noticing.

## Decision

The resolution proof's hash is bound into the envelope's additional authenticated data, so that a misresolved envelope refuses to open. Binding, not accompaniment. RECORD (Sonic, carried in the 2026-09-04 handoff).

## Alternatives considered

- Carry the proof beside the envelope as a sibling field — rejected: a field can be replaced and the ciphertext still opens.
- Sign the proof separately — rejected: adds a key and still does not fail closed.

## Consequences

Sets the shape of §7.2 and gives P-1 its first clause. The sizing objection this raised — a full proof will not fit inside an AAD — was answered by D-26 (proof by reference) and the composition fixed by D-53. The AAD's key names were fixed at the freeze by D-127.
