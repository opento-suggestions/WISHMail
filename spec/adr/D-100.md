## Context

The object and the act shared a word — a stamp, and stamping — and the token had no name at all.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-100, verbatim:

> **The token is `$POSTAGE`**; one unit is a stamp. Christened by Sonic 09-06 to separate the object (a stamp) from the act (stamping). §2.2 (postage), §4.1 amended (`CHANGED`).

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

The token is **`$POSTAGE`** and one unit of it is a stamp. Christened by Sonic to separate the object from the act. §2.2 gains *postage* as a term — stamps as the price of carriage — and §4.1 names the token. No token other than the stamp is postage, at `send` or at replay (T-P11-1).
