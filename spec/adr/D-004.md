## Context

Registries disagree. A protocol that picks a winner becomes the authority it was built to avoid being.

## Decision

WISHMail reads registries and adjudicates between none. Where two disagree about one address, the envelope names the profile it resolved through and commits that resolution; the disagreement is a recorded fact, not a verdict. RECORD.

## Alternatives considered

- Rank registries by trust — rejected: the ranking is the moat.
- Require one registry — rejected: violates what became P-5.

## Consequences

Becomes P-6 and the second half of P-5. §11.6 makes disagreement between surfaces an observation, never an adjudication.
