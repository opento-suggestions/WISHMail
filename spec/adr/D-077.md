## Context

The acknowledgment window needed a ceiling, and long-term scheduled transactions had to actually be live for D-76 to work at all.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-77, verbatim:

> HIP-423 long-term scheduled transactions are live on mainnet (since network release 0.57; FETCHED 09-05). The acknowledgment window is the schedule's expiration, at most the network's maximum lifetime, pinned as `SCHEDULE_MAX_LIFETIME` = 5,356,800 s (62 days), verified by Sonic 09-05 against docs.hedera.com (scheduled transactions). Closes v0.3 §19 item 14b. Nothing is deferred.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

FETCHED 2026-09-05: HIP-423 long-term schedules are live on mainnet since network release 0.57, and `SCHEDULE_MAX_LIFETIME` is 5,356,800 seconds — 62 days. The window is the schedule's expiration, a sender parameter at most that maximum and defaulting to it (§10.4). A schedule that expires unsigned is deleted by the network and reconciliation reports `unclaimed` (§11.4, T-P15-5). Pinned in §1.6 as a network fact.
