## Context

A purchase in HBAR has to bind the quoted amount to the buyer's signature, or the Postmaster could alter it after signing.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-106, verbatim:

> **`hbar` leg principles, from the qisma study (re-derived, not copied):** the transaction ID names the Postmaster as payer (unsubmittable without it); valid duration bounds the quote; the buyer signs in its own process and stops; a Hedera signature covers the whole body, so an altered amount is a transaction the buyer never signed. New MUST + T-P13-3. Dedup record made **durable across restarts** (the study's replay defences were in-memory Sets) — T-P11-5 revised. `rate`: the buyer signs the quoted amount; the cap lives in the signed artifact, so rate movement inside the valid duration is the Postmaster's risk. qisma performs no two-asset swap (its facilitator forbids token transfers) — WISHMail's atomic two-asset transfer needs no precedent: a native Hedera CryptoTransfer, two signers, net zero in two assets. `upto` is not specified for Hedera and Hedera `exact` requires the exact amount — a Hedera cap would need an allowance or escrow primitive (§16 candidate, SPEC not BUILD).

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

Principles re-derived from the qisma study, not copied (Start Fresh, D-13): the transaction ID names the Postmaster as payer, so it cannot be submitted without it; the valid duration bounds how long the quote stands; the buyer signs in its own process and stops; a Hedera signature covers the whole body, so an altered amount is a transaction the buyer never signed and the network rejects it (§14.2, T-P13-3). One transaction, atomic — HBAR out, stamps in, whole or not at all.
