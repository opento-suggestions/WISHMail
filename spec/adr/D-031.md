## Context

Which of the six verbs may cost money, and on what principle.

## Decision

Only `buy_stamp` is priced by the specification. `verify` is free as a verb, forever: anyone may replay the topics and appraise without paying anyone. A narrative service may charge for the reading; it may not charge for the evidence, because the evidence is public. RECORD, 2026-09-04; the PAEA split.

## Alternatives considered

- Meter verification — rejected: it contradicts P-3 and P-4 outright.

## Consequences

Becomes §4.7's two services — stamping market-dominant, reconciliation competitive — and §14.1's "money enters WISHMail in one place". §11.7's narrative MUST is the one constraint on the competitive tier.
