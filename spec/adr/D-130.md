## Context

L-13 said postage is 'consumed when affixed' while five other places put consumption at SETTLED.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-130, verbatim:

> **Postage is spent at affix and consumed at settlement** — L-13 said "consumed when affixed" while §2.2, §4.3, §8.8, §14.4 and §17.10 place consumption at SETTLED and spending at affix. L-13 now reads "spent when affixed and consumed when the envelope settles." No test changes.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

L-13 now reads spent-at-affix and consumed-at-settlement, agreeing with §2.2, §4.3, §8.8, §14.4 and §17.10. The distinction is what makes an orphan not a loss (F-3, D-52): postage on an envelope not yet posted is spent, and the stamp is not consumed until the envelope reaches the lane.
