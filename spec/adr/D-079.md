## Context

Three of the four proofs have manifests. Whether the envelope itself needed one, and where it would go, was open.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-79, verbatim:

> The proof of posting is implicit: chunk 0 is its manifest, the AAD its output, the postmark its witness, and the envelope identifier stands for its hash wherever a later proof consumes it. No separate manifest is published.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

No separate manifest is published. Chunk 0 **is** the manifest of the proof of posting, the AAD is its output, the postmark is its witness, and the envelope identifier stands for its hash wherever a later proof consumes it (§10.3). This is why the header must rebuild to `id`: the proof and the object are the same bytes.
