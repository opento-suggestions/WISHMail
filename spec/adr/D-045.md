## Context

The design had verbs but no account of which actor performs which, and 'delivery' was being used for two different events.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-45, verbatim:

> Three acts: Assembly → Delivery → Reconciliation. Assembler binds, Consensus witnesses, Verifier reconciles, Postmaster carries. Delivery is to the lane; only a return receipt says a recipient opened.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

§3.2's four verbs and §8.4's three acts. Assembly is everything the sender does, delivery is the instant of SETTLED, reconciliation is a reading that writes nothing back. Delivery is to the lane and never to a hand; only a return receipt says a recipient opened (§2.3).
