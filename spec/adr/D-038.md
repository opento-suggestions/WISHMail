## Context

WISHMail's proofs are instances of an Ontologic primitive, and the primitive has a name. Using that name normatively would make the specification depend on a paper.

## Decision

The postal words are the normative vocabulary. *Morpheme* and *morpheme proof* appear colloquially and in informative text only, with the paper as an informative reference. Demonstration, not advertisement. RECORD, 2026-09-04.

## Alternatives considered

- Use the Ontologic vocabulary normatively — rejected: a reader who never opens the paper must lose nothing normative.

## Consequences

Becomes §2.3's reservation ("*Proof* is the normative word ... *Morpheme* ... appears in §2.4 and in informative text only") and §2.4's correspondence table, which binds nothing. The postal motifs used in working sessions appear in the ledger and never in the specification (CLAUDE.md §8).
