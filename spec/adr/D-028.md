## Context

The earlier handoff put the return receipt somewhere other than the connection topic; the correction was that it belongs where the correspondence is.

## Decision

The return receipt lands on the connection topic — the lane. RECORD, 2026-09-04; corrects handoff §5.2 item 5.

## Alternatives considered

- Land it on the recipient's outbound log — rejected at the time: the lane is where the correspondence is.

## Consequences

Superseded in mechanism. §10.4 as it stands lands the receipt's manifest on the *recipient's manifest topic*, by the execution of a scheduled submission (D-76, D-78); what the lane carries is the *request* — HCS-10's `transaction` operation naming the schedule. The reason the receipt moved off the lane is attribution: a lane's submit key is a threshold of two keys, so consensus records that one of them signed and not which, while a submission to the recipient's manifest topic is under the recipient's key alone. What this ADR got right and 0.5.0 keeps is that the exchange happens on the lane.
