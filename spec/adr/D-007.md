## Context

If postage varied by the registry an address resolved through, WISHMail would be pricing registries, which is adjudicating between them by another route.

## Decision

A stamp costs the same regardless of which registry resolved the recipient. One published price schedule, identical for every buyer, independent of profile, address, or recipient. RECORD.

## Alternatives considered

- Price by registry — rejected: it is D-4 by the back door.
- Price by content or sender — rejected: the carrier does not read.

## Consequences

Becomes P-11 and §4.5. The schedule was later put on consensus by D-101; the numbers stay a deployment's (Q-6, §19.2).
