## Context

The cross-ledger ambition needed a line between a chain WISHMail might one day carry mail on and a place WISHMail might be shown.

## Decision

Ocean Protocol is a venue, not a CLPR target ledger. RECORD.

## Alternatives considered

- Name it as a target ledger — rejected: it would put a ledger tag in §5.1 with nothing behind it.

## Consequences

No normative section. §5.1 and D-114 define exactly two tags, and §16.7 says what an extension for a third would have to pin.
