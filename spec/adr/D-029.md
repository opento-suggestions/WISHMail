## Context

Whether every envelope should be acknowledged, and who should pay for it.

## Decision

The base service is blind delivery. A return receipt is an option the sender requests and funds at `send`; the recipient pays nothing to acknowledge. Scheduled transactions are the candidate mechanism (Q-14). RECORD, 2026-09-04.

## Alternatives considered

- Acknowledge everything — rejected: it makes reading a lane a write, and P-14 a lie.
- Charge the recipient — rejected: it becomes P-16.

## Consequences

Becomes `hdr.rr` (§7.7), the receipt fee of one stamp inside postage (D-59), and P-16's second clause (T-P16-2). Reading a lane leaves no mark on it (§6.5). Q-14 was closed by D-78: the mechanism is a scheduled submission the recipient signs.
