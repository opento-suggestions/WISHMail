## Context

A Verifier reads through a mirror node, and a mirror can lie or omit. Whether that is defended, and how far, had to be stated rather than assumed.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-84, verbatim:

> Mirror independence is a MUST (T-P4-3): evidence never depends on which mirror node was read. Running-hash recomputation and sequence-continuity checks are MAY, reported under `observations.integrity`; the ledger's running-hash construction is a FETCH before any of it can bind (G.8).

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

Mirror independence is a MUST: evidence never depends on which mirror was read (§11.1, T-P4-3). Running-hash recomputation and sequence continuity are MAY, reported under `observations.integrity` — a Verifier performing them is checking its mirror, not the correspondence, and a mirror that fails is read around, not proven false (§11.6). Stated as L-10; the v3 construction is FETCHED in ledger §H for whoever implements it.
