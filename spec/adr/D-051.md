## Context

Two payments needed a mechanism: the fee to ring a stranger's doorbell, and the postage on an envelope.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-51, verbatim:

> Consumption: HIP-991 on the doorbell (one stamp, collected by the recipient); on the lane, a single sender-signed transfer of postage to the treasury with the AAD hash in the memo. Affixed at the transfer, consumed at SETTLED; burn is optional bookkeeping.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

The affixing transfer of §4.3 stands as written: one sender-signed transfer of the envelope's postage to the treasury, carrying the AAD hash in its memo, affixed at the transfer and consumed at SETTLED. **The doorbell half did not survive**: this row collects the fee for the recipient, and D-105 rules that the fee is consumed by the treasury — a recipient is not paid to be written to. §4.4 and T-P7-4 read the treasury as collector.
