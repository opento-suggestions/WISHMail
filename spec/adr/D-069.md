## Context

Four surfaces were being drafted with four different declaration shapes, which would have made an agent declare four different things about itself.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-69, verbatim:

> §9: four profiles — `hcs14` (native; math), `dns` (social-committee), `agentfacts` (social-committee), `hol` (social-committee + withheld); A2A is §16. One Declaration object, three surfaces: HCS-11 `properties.wishmail`; a `_wishmail.<fqdn>` TXT record in DKIM/DMARC tag=value form (`v=wm1; l; a; d; o; k; e`); an AgentFacts top-level `wishmail` field (the Data Facts single-field precedent).

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

**One Declaration object, several surfaces** (§9.1) — that half stands and is what makes cross-registry addressing coherent. **Two halves did not survive the recon**: the `agentfacts` profile is not what the live NANDA index serves, and D-97/D-98 replace it with `nanda` keyed to the v2 index; and `hol` is not social-committee with `withheld` — D-104 re-founds it on the consensus anchors as `math` with `blurred`, since reads are free and unauthenticated so nothing is withheld.
