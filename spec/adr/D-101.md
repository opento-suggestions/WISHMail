## Context

A price the Postmaster asserts at purchase time is a price a buyer cannot check afterward, and a Verifier cannot check at all.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-101, verbatim:

> **The price list lives on consensus.** A PriceList is a message on the Postmaster's price topic (memo `wishmail:prices:1`, Postmaster's sole submit key); the price current at a purchase is the latest message before it; read from a mirror at every purchase; the claim names the topic as `prices {ledgerTag, topicId}`. The schedule may carry `bundles` (a price for a count) and, for a method priced by reference, a `rate {source, pair}` read at purchase and recorded on the receipt. §4.5 says "price schedule"; §14.3 rewritten; T-P11-4 now compares against the message current at the receipt's consensus timestamp — so P-11 is checkable historically by any Verifier. Sonic 09-06. Consumption stated in §14.4 per §4.3 (to treasury under the envelope's memo, the cancellation).

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

The price list is a message on the Postmaster's price topic — memo `wishmail:prices:1`, sole submit key the Postmaster's — and the price current at a purchase is the latest message with an earlier consensus timestamp. The Postmaster reads it from a mirror at every purchase, and a Verifier reads the same message to check what was charged (§14.3, T-P11-4). The numbers stay a deployment's (Q-6, §19.2); what the specification fixes is that there is one schedule, on consensus before it is charged, the same for everyone.
