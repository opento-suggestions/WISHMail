## Context

§4.4 requires a doorbell to carry a HIP-991 fee of exactly one stamp collected by the treasury, and says two things about what that costs whom:

> The recipient is paid nothing to be knocked on and owes nothing to answer; what it receives is the request. **First contact therefore costs one stamp to ring** and, once the lane exists, the envelope's weight to send, and every one of those stamps is consumed.

Standing up the reference Postmaster's own doorbell raised the question of who submits what to it. FETCHED 2026-09-07 from the pinned HCS-10 blob — `hiero-ledger/hiero-consensus-specifications` @ `7046156c`, `docs/standards/hcs-10/index.md`, blob `0cb5d2eb…`, whose git-hash and SHA-256 both reproduce `spec/pins.json` exactly:

> **`index.md:498`** — "Sent by an agent **on its own Inbound Topic** in response to a `connection_request`, confirming the creation of a new Connection Topic and providing its ID."

with its transaction memo at `:522`, `hcs-10:op:4:1` — operation 4 (`connection_created`) on topic type 1 (inbound), per the enum at `:362` that §6.1 tells WISHMail to use. The `hcs-10:op:4:2` form at `:576` is the separate *record* on the confirming agent's outbound topic, not the answer.

So the recipient answers on its own fee-gated doorbell. Unexempted, it pays one stamp to answer — and §4.4's "owes nothing to answer" is false. Worse, and harder to argue with: **first contact then costs two stamps**, one to ring and one to answer, and §4.4's own arithmetic is false with it.

§4.4 grants the remedy but does not require it: "An agent MAY exempt keys from its doorbell's fee." T-P7-4's row said nothing about it, so a doorbell that charged its owner to answer passed the suite.

## Decision

T-P7-4's row in §A is amended to test the **outcome**, against the sentence that already binds — not to turn §4.4's MAY into a fixture MUST. It now ends:

> … and an owner's `connection_created` on its own doorbell assesses the owner zero stamps (§4.4: the recipient "owes nothing to answer").

Exempting the owner's key at creation is *how* a doorbell satisfies this, and the test does not name the mechanism. The provisioning template does (D-138).

Sonic, 2026-09-07: "Do not turn §4.4's exemption MAY into a fixture MUST. Test the outcome instead, against the sentence that already binds … The ADR records that the sketch under-tested an already-normative sentence." RECORD.

**Not a version change.** §1.7 makes a test change a minor bump "at least" when it "would invalidate a previously conforming implementation." No implementation conforms — T-P9-2 blocks every claim while `spec/pins.json` carries unfilled pins — so there is nothing to invalidate. And no requirement is added: the requirement was already in §4.4, and only the row was silent.

## Alternatives considered

- **Amend §4.4 to make the owner's exemption a MUST.** Rejected by Sonic. The outcome is what §4.4 already promises; mandating one mechanism forecloses others (a doorbell whose owner is exempt by a threshold key, say) and would be a spec change where a test correction suffices.
- **Leave T-P7-4 as it stands and rely on the provisioning template.** Rejected: the template is this deployment's, and the register is what every implementation is tried against. A conforming doorbell that charged its owner to answer would contradict §4.4 while passing.
- **Treat "owes nothing to answer" as loose prose and change nothing.** Rejected on the arithmetic, which is not loose: §4.4 states first contact costs one stamp, and unexempted it costs two.

## Consequences

The suite gains a check with a concrete read path. `GET /api/v1/transactions/{id}` exposes `assessed_custom_fees[{amount, collector_account_id, effective_payer_account_ids, token_id}]` (ledger §H), so the property is read the same way the rest of T-P7-4 is: the owner's `connection_created` shows an empty `assessed_custom_fees`, and a stranger's `connection_request` shows one stamp assessed to the treasury.

The mechanism cooperates without any further requirement. HIP-991 waives **by signature** — "If a TopicMessageSubmitTransaction … contains a signature from a key included in the Fee Exempt Key List, no fees will be charged for that message" (`hip-991.md:110-113`, pinned by D-135) — and §3.5 already has the agent sign its own submissions while the Postmaster pays. So the exemption applies even where the Postmaster is the transaction payer, and D-47 is untouched.

**Recorded upstream, for L-7.** HCS-10 mentions fee exemption **nowhere**: zero occurrences of "exempt" in the pinned blob. It offers the fee-gated inbound option (`index.md:113`) without addressing that the topic's owner must answer on the same topic. That is a gap in HCS-10, not only in this document, and it belongs with the other Draft-dependence findings.
