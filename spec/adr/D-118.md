## Decision

> **§17 postal grounding drafted** (MINE throughout; awaiting approval). Eleven subsections, informative, no requirement: the letter (39 CFR §310.1 → P-10); the mailbox is not a public forum (*Greenburgh* → P-7, doorbell fee; lane fee-free as "inside the house"); uniform postage and universal service (39 U.S.C. §101 → P-11, §3.5's acceptance rule — "the registry is WISHMail's geography"); the two tiers (PAEA, 39 U.S.C. §§3621/3631 → §4.7; the extremely-urgent price floor → Q-11: service classes priced above the stamp, never below); the carrier does not read and cannot hide (*Konan* inverted → P-2/P-3: no immunity needed because no private record); certified mail / return receipt / attempted-delivery notice / the box (→ §10, §1.4 RECIPIENT as PO box); private hands (PES exemption → §1.2 non-goal: obligation taken, monopoly refused); one postal territory (UPU Constitution art. 1 → P-5/P-6, §16.7: registries are addressing systems, ledgers are administrations); what a stamp does not fund (PAEA pre-funding / PSRA → §14.4, L-14); a term concordance. Sources: `RESEARCH_USPS_PES_2026-09-04.md` (RECORD of a report Sonic pasted 09-04; its sourcing FETCHED-unverified) plus Claude's training for the UPU, the USC section numbers, and the letter-carrier terms — **nothing in §17 was fetched this session (network off)**; the citation register is G.4.
