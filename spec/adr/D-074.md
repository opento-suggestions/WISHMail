## Context

Three words were overloaded: the proof's fourth part, the document a release publishes, and what an agent puts on a registry.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-74, verbatim:

> Terminology: the proof's fourth part is **meaning** (the paper's M), the release's document is a **conformance claim**, and **declaration / declare** belong to an agent publishing under a profile. *Declared* vs *appraised* (P-12) is a proof's standing, stated vs established.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

The proof's fourth part is **meaning** (the paper's M). The release's document is a **conformance claim**. **Declaration** and *to declare* belong to an agent publishing under a profile. *Declared* against *appraised* (P-12) is a different pair — two acts by two parties sharing a root only (§2.3). This reserve is what D-134 later enforced against a stale test sketch.
