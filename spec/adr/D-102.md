## Context

Two repositories carry an x402 specification and one of them is a fork, so 'pinned to x402' had to name which.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-102, verbatim:

> **`buy_stamp` over `x402-usdc` is BUILD** (Sonic 09-06). x402 pinned: `x402-foundation/x402` @ `0c04a84e…` is the normative repo (`coinbase/x402` is a self-declared development fork, 480 commits behind, carrying a Hedera scheme blob that predates the 2026-07-03 payer-signature hardening — never read it); spec v2, HTTP transport, `exact`, and `exact`-Hedera pinned in §1.6. §14.2 says the network is CAIP-2 from the price list; a non-Hedera network is the P-16 leg; on a Hedera network the buyer signs a USDC transfer and the facilitator co-signs as fee payer; the Postmaster keeps its own settled-reference record (T-P11-5) because the Hedera scheme's replay rule is a SHOULD.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

FETCHED 2026-09-06: `x402-foundation/x402` is the normative repository; `coinbase/x402` is a self-declared development fork, 480 commits behind, carrying a Hedera scheme blob that predates the 2026-07-03 payer-signature hardening. Four blobs pinned at `0c04a84e`: the v2 core, the HTTP transport, the `exact` scheme, and the Hedera `exact` scheme (§1.6). `buy_stamp` over `x402-usdc` is BUILD. The facilitator that settles it was named later, by D-132.
