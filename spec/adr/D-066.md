## Context

State was being used for two different things — how far an envelope had travelled, and whether what travelled binds — and conflating them would have let a well-formed envelope look delivered when it was unstamped.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-66, verbatim:

> §8: two machines (lane: NONE→REQUESTED→OPEN→CLOSED; envelope: DRAFT→STAMPED→SUBMITTED→SETTLED→ACKED) on two axes (state, standing); consensus time the only clock; states monotone; v0.2's RESOLVED is not an envelope state but the existence of a reading; v0.2's REQUESTED is a lane state, not an envelope state.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

Two axes: **state** (DRAFT → STAMPED → SUBMITTED → SETTLED → ACKED) and **standing** (§11.5), computed separately and reported together. An envelope can be fully SETTLED and unstamped; a receipt can be witnessed and invalid. Two machines: the lane's and the envelope's. Consensus time is the only clock and states are monotone (§8.1). v0.2's RESOLVED is not an envelope state.
