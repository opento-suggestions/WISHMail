## Context

Two documents were doing one job: a consolidation ledger recording how the design got here, and a specification saying what WISHMail is.

## Decision

v0.3 is a historical ledger, not the normative document. The specification after it is authoritative: indicative register, RFC 2119, no MUST without a conformance test. The BUILD SET lives in the repository's STATUS, not in the specification. Build versioning starts independently. RECORD, 2026-09-04.

## Alternatives considered

- Keep one document doing both — rejected: a specification that narrates its own history cannot be read as a specification.

## Consequences

Becomes the register discipline of the whole document and CLAUDE.md §1's reading order. Two carried clauses changed: the authoritative text is 0.5.0, not v0.4 (D-125), and v0.3 *is* in the repository — as provenance only, binding nothing (CLAUDE.md §1 item 4), because the ADRs backfilled from it need their source at hand.
