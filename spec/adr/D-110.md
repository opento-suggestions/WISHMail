## Context

The testnet HOL anchor has no keys at all, so any account may submit a registration to it — which means an agent can anchor itself under its own key rather than the broker's.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-110, verbatim:

> **Be the first: self-registration on the keyless testnet anchor is BUILD** (Sonic 09-06). §4.6 (`CHANGED`): where an anchor admits any account, provisioning MAY prepare the agent's registration for the *agent* to submit as payer under its own key, and MAY fund the fee; the agent then resolves under `hol` without `blurred`. The Postmaster never signs or submits it — HCS-10's `register` has no signature field and the mirror exposes no signature map, so the payer is the only authorship signal on consensus (Claude's first draft had the Postmaster paying, which would have carried `blurred` by §9.5's own rule; corrected same turn). T-P13-4.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

Where an anchor admits any account, provisioning MAY prepare the agent's registration for the **agent** to submit as payer under its own key, and MAY fund the fee. The registration is the agent's, not the Postmaster's, which never signs or submits it. Such an agent resolves under `hol` without `blurred`, because the account that paid for the registration is the account it names (§4.6, T-P13-4).
