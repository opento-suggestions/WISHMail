## Context

A slip asserts an absence, and an absence published after the fact by an interested party proves nothing unless it is witnessed with everything it rests on.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-80, verbatim:

> `send` publishes the slip's manifest on the sender's manifest topic before returning a slip; a slip's output (`unanswered` by T+window) is recomputable from the doorbell; slips are never superseded.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

`send` publishes the slip's manifest on the sender's manifest topic **before** returning a slip (§10.5, T-P12-5). The slip's output — `unanswered` by the request's timestamp plus the window — is recomputable by any Verifier reading the doorbell for a `connection_created` and finding none. A slip is never superseded: a lane that opens later is OPEN, and the slip stays true of the window it names.
