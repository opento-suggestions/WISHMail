## Context

The specification, its tests, and its reference implementation can live apart or together. Apart, they drift.

## Decision

One monorepo in three parts: `spec/` normative, `conformance/` the suite, `app/` the reference implementation. RECORD; mirroring the agentrust-io posture.

## Alternatives considered

- Separate repositories per part — rejected: a schema change without a spec change becomes invisible.

## Consequences

Becomes §18.5 and CLAUDE.md §4. The chain of custody it implies — spec leads, schema follows, tests are the court — is stated in §18.5's last line and in §5.11 (a schema change without a specification change is not a change to WISHMail).
