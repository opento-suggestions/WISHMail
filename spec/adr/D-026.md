## Context

D-1 put the resolution proof inside the AAD, and a full proof does not fit inside one.

## Decision

What enters the AAD is the proof's hash. The proof itself — its manifest — lives behind the hash and is dereferenced; the decompiler is built into the application. A proof travels by reference: an object naming one carries `{hash, uri}`. RECORD, 2026-09-04.

## Alternatives considered

- Inline a reduced proof — rejected: a reduced proof is not the proof, and its hash would not recompute.

## Consequences

Becomes §5.2's by-reference rule and §7.2's `rp`. Where the manifest is published was decided after: D-55 put it on consensus before `send`, and D-73 gave every agent a dedicated manifest topic.
