## Context

A narrative is prose, and prose can say anything. Binding it to the evidence without over-constraining a competitive service was the problem.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-85, verbatim:

> The narrative's one MUST: it carries the digest of the evidence it reads (T-P3-4). "States nothing the bundle does not contain" is SHOULD; the reference narrative is generated from fixed templates over bundle fields so the SHOULD is demonstrated. The competitive tier is bound by the digest and nothing else in §11.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

One MUST: the narrative carries the digest of the evidence it reads, so a reader can check the reading against what it claims to read (§11.7, T-P3-4). 'States nothing the bundle does not contain' is a SHOULD, demonstrated rather than promised — the reference narrative is generated from fixed templates over bundle fields. A narrative service of the competitive tier (§4.7) is bound by the MUST and by nothing else.
