## Context

D-76 named ScheduleSign; who creates the schedule, who pays, where the inner transaction writes, and what the lane carries were all still open.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-78, verbatim:

> Return receipt, composed: after SETTLED the sender creates a schedule whose inner transaction submits the pre-filled receipt manifest to the *recipient's* manifest topic (sole key: recipient's), payer the Postmaster, and posts HCS-10's `transaction` op on the lane naming it; `ack` = ScheduleSign; execution publishes the manifest. Attribution by the schedule's signature (mirror `schedules` endpoint exposes signatures and executed_timestamp — FETCHED-class). Unsigned expiry = `unclaimed`, not refused. Closes Q-14. Reconciles D-73 and D-76.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

After SETTLED the sender creates the schedule with the receipt manifest pre-filled, its inner transaction submitting to the **recipient's** manifest topic (sole key: the recipient's), payer the Postmaster (D-47), `waitForExpiry` false; it then posts the `transaction` operation on the lane. `ack` is a ScheduleSign, and execution is instant. The recipient pays nothing at any step (T-P16-2). This is what superseded carried D-28, which had put the receipt itself on the lane.
