## Context

A hackathon-window build needs a line between what WISHMail is and what it merely touches.

## Decision

WISHMail is three things — resolve, postage, consensus — and everything else is sorted into four buckets: Core, Profile, Channel, Venue. RECORD.

## Alternatives considered

- An open-ended product surface — rejected: nothing would be finishable or testable.

## Consequences

§1.1 states the three; §1.2 states the refusals. The bucket vocabulary itself does not appear in 0.5.0; the line it drew does.
