## Context

A lane's submit key is a threshold of two keys, so consensus records that one of the two signed and not which. Without something else, an envelope on a lane has no attributable sender.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-64, verbatim:

> The fourth weld: the sender of an envelope is the `from` account of its settlement (network-enforced signature, memo names the envelope). `operator_id` MUST equal `settlement.from`. Survives key rotation.

## Alternatives considered

- Put a sender field in the header — rejected: a header field is written by whoever writes the chunk, and both parties can write.

## Consequences

The sender is the `from` account of the envelope's settlement: a token transfer the network accepted only under that account's key, carrying this envelope's identifier in its memo. `operator_id` MUST equal `settlement.from` (§7.2, T-P1-6). It survives key rotation — every envelope an account ever affixed stays attributed to it (§7.6, F-8).
