## Context

Four standings need an order, and 'why' needs to be answerable without a Verifier writing prose.

## Decision

The record, from ledger §B row D-88, verbatim:

> Standing order **verified > unverified > unstamped > unbound**; the envelope's standing is the lowest any check yields; every reason is the identifier of the conformance test whose fixture exercises the condition; an envelope with no reasons is verified; the receipt's standing is reported beside and never lowers the envelope's; an unnamed condition reports unverified with reason `T-P12-2` (a spec defect). Confirmed by Sonic 09-05.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded. The ledger §B row states the decision without a fork; where a fork was closed, the row says so.

## Consequences

`verified > unverified > unstamped > unbound`; an envelope's standing is the lowest any check yields; an envelope with no reasons is verified. **Every reason is the identifier of a conformance test** — there is no reason without a test, as there is no requirement without one (§1.3). A condition §11.5's table does not name is a spec defect, reported unverified with reason `T-P12-2`. The receipt is reported beside and never lowers the envelope's standing.
