## Context

A service that carries envelopes and takes money needs its trust posture stated, or a reader will assume the worst or the best.

## Decision

The Postmaster's trust class is that of a courier: `math`. It transports bytes and attests nothing. Its blast radius is delay only. RECORD, 2026-09-04: "let's name the Postmaster's trust class explicitly".

## Alternatives considered

- Leave it unstated — rejected: the whole of P-2 is what this class means in practice.

## Consequences

Becomes §3.5 and P-2. Its mechanism came with D-47 (the Postmaster pays, the agent signs, and holds no key on any agent topic) and its bound with the §13 F-7 landing: one stamp per abandoned first contact, and nothing more.
